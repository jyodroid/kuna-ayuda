## English (default)

**Short description**
```
Earthquakes & wildfires, help points, mutual aid and offline SOS.
```

**Full description**
```
Kuna Ayuda brings together, in one place, what's useful during a disaster — starting with earthquakes and wildfires, and prioritizing the affected areas.

What you can do:
• Live earthquake feed, ranked by the regions likely affected, with aftershocks.
• Active wildfire feed, showing intensity near populated areas.
• Official help points on a map — verified shelters and collection centers, with directions and a "near me" view.
• Mutual-aid network — request or offer resources between neighbors (moderated).
• Lost & Found — reunification reports for people and pets, with photos.
• Geolocated SOS — call for help with your location, or check in as safe. It works offline: your alert is saved and sent automatically when you regain signal.
• Emergency beacon — flash your phone's light and sound an SOS pattern so rescuers can find you, even with no connection.
• Offline guide — official emergency numbers and safety tips, available without internet.

Available for Colombia, Indonesia, Spain and Italy, with more countries to come. Localized in Spanish, English, Bahasa Indonesia and Italian.

Privacy first: you can use the app anonymously — no account, no ads, no tracking. You share information only when you choose to (for example, your location in an SOS).

Kuna Ayuda is not an emergency service. In an emergency, always call your country's official numbers (for example 123 in Colombia, 112 in Spain, Italy and Indonesia). Community content is unverified — verify before acting.

Kuna Ayuda is a free, open-source, public-good project.
```

## App content → Target audience and content

**Target age groups:** select **18 and over** (primary). Optionally also 16–17 and 13–15 if you want
teens included. **Do NOT check any under-13 group.**
- Why: checking an under-13 group puts the app in Google's **Families / "Designed for Families"** program,
  which forbids/limits location collection and imposes strict UGC and ads rules — incompatible with SOS
  location + the aid board. Targeting 18+ (or 13+) keeps it a standard app.

**Are children (under 13) in your target audience?** — **No**

**Could your app unintentionally appeal to children?** — **No**
- It's a disaster-relief utility; no cartoonish/child-appealing characters, games, or themes.

**Store listing — is it designed to attract children?** — **No**

**Ads shown to children?** — **N/A** (the app has no ads).

**Result:** the app is NOT in the Designed for Families program; standard policies apply. This is the
correct posture for an app that collects location and hosts user content.
